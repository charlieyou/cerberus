Strategy: decompose.
Break the artifact into its constituent parts — sections, subsystems, requirements, files, or commits as appropriate to the review type — and judge each part on its own merits before forming a view of the whole.
Surface findings at the granularity of the part they apply to (name the section, the requirement ID, the function, or the file) rather than at the artifact level, so each finding is locally actionable.
A part may be sound while its neighbors are not, and a part may be unsound while the artifact's overall direction is fine; do not let a verdict on the whole smear over either case.
Where parts interact, call out the interaction explicitly as its own finding rather than folding it into a per-part note.
